1. PROJECT NAME
==================================================

Project name: LinkUp

LinkUp is a startup-style web platform that connects brands with event organizers and eventually other partnership participants.

Core idea:

BRANDS ↔ EVENTS

Brands need:
- Sponsorship opportunities
- Events to promote their products
- Access to relevant audiences
- Product activation opportunities
- Brand partnerships
- Collaborations
- Promotional opportunities

Event organizers need:
- Sponsors
- Brands
- Products
- Services
- Promotional support
- Vendors
- Collaborators
- Partnership opportunities

LinkUp acts as the platform that helps both sides discover each other, connect, communicate, propose partnerships, and eventually manage collaborations.

The long-term vision is NOT just a sponsorship website. It should evolve into a broader partnership marketplace/network.

Possible future network:

Brands
Events
Creators
Communities
Service Providers
